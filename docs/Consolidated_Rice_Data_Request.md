Subject: Request for matched trial and molecular records for rice yield forecasting research

Dear Dr. Bartholomé,

I am conducting a rice yield forecasting research project at Birla Institute of Technology, Mesra, in the Department of Quantitative Economics and Data Science, under the guidance of Dr. Manish Kumar Pandey.

Our question is whether information available before harvest can predict rice yield per hectare and low-yield risk in held-out environments, and whether adding verified genotype-linked protein sequence embeddings improves prediction beyond weather and genetic markers. Protein information would be an explanatory input; we are not forecasting protein structures. We would include aluminium as a predictor only where matched measurements support it.

We have acquired and audited the public supplements for your 2023 study, Multi-environment Genomic Selection in Rice Elite Breeding Lines (DOI 10.1186/s12284-023-00623-6), and supporting elite-panel data from Juma et al. (2021). Our files contain 107 genotyped lines and 15 environments. At the 66 shared GIDs, all 44,948 comparable nucleotide calls agree between the two sources. We have also acquired NASA POWER weather at the published trial coordinates.

Could you please advise whether the following matched records exist, and provide a repository location, access procedure, or academic-use data if available?

1. Molecular identity and sequences: a crosswalk from the published GIDs to sequenced sample/accession IDs; assembly and transcript versions; full gene/protein allele sequences or sufficiently complete variant calls for stress-response and yield-related genes. If available, records for Ptr and aluminium-response genes such as STAR1/STAR2 would help. We have 38 candidate Ptr name matches among the yield genotypes from a separate published study (DOI 10.1002/tpg2.70290), but cannot confirm sample identity or full alleles from those names alone. Neither of its two diagnostic positions appears in the acquired 882-marker panel. Please indicate the appropriate collaborator if these molecular records are maintained elsewhere.

2. Trial soil and aluminium: measurements matched to the attached environment IDs, including sample/plot identifiers, dates/depths, units/methods, pH, texture, organic carbon, nutrients and, if measured, exchangeable aluminium or aluminium saturation. Individual samples would be preferable to summaries, but either would help establish availability.

3. Management and forecast timing: fertiliser formulations, amounts and application dates; irrigation regime/amounts; planting/transplanting, sampling and harvest dates; and any field weather observations. These records are needed to distinguish variables available at forecast time from later observations.

4. Original outcomes and experimental design: plot-level grain yields, harvested areas, moisture corrections, replicates/blocks and spatial coordinates, plus adjustment methods and uncertainties. If crop stress/disease severity or damage was recorded, its dates, definitions and measurements would help us determine whether a damage target is feasible. We will not equate low yield with measured damage without such evidence.

5. Data clarifications: GID 4016512 (IRRI 154) has two different YLD entries in each of Gazipur, Nizmawna, Chokwe, Maputo and Dakawa in Additional file 4. Could you clarify their identity or experimental roles? Could you also identify which Hyderabad trial used unadjusted phenotypes in the unreplicated design?

6. Optional CIRAD aluminium case-study clarification: for DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0307009, if you can direct us to the data custodian, could we obtain individual soil-sample/plot mappings, exact sowing/harvest dates and confirmation of harvested area? The downloaded grain-weight/moisture/YLD values imply approximately 1.50 m2, while the nominal row dimensions imply 1.56 m2. We have preserved the published yields and would appreciate clarification of the convention. We will keep this population separate from the elite-panel trials.

If any requested variables were not measured, confirmation would help us set a defensible scope. We welcome a smaller linked subset with reliable records rather than unverified assignments. We would follow access and usage conditions, preserve originals, and cite the studies and data providers. We do not need bulk raw reads where validated variant calls or sequences are available.
